Methodology

This analysis applies Benjamin Graham's time-tested value investing principles:

  • Graham Number: Maximum price based on P/E ≤ 15 and P/B ≤ 1.5
  • Net-Net NCAV: Buy when price < 2/3 of net current asset value
  • Normalized Earnings: Value based on 7-10 year average earnings
  • Dividend-Adjusted: Adjusts for growth and interest rates

Target Margin of Safety: 30% minimum for defensive investors, 50%+ for enterprising investors

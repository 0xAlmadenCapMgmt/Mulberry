Methodology

Valuation Methods

Earnings-Book Value
√(22.5 × EPS × BVPS)
Maximum price consistent with P/E ≤ 15 and P/B ≤ 1.5 simultaneously
Net Asset Value
(Current Assets − Total Liab.) / Shares × ⅔
Liquidation floor — strong buy signal when price trades below working capital
Normalized Earnings Power
Avg EPS × (8.5 + 2g)
Value based on multi-year average EPS to smooth cyclical earnings volatility
Growth-Adjusted Earnings
(EPS × (8.5 + 2g) × 4.4) / Y
Adjusts for expected earnings growth and prevailing corporate bond yields

Margin of Safety = (Intrinsic Value − Market Price) / Intrinsic Value. A margin of ≥ 30% indicates sufficient buffer against estimation error for value investing. A margin of ≥ 50% represents a deep value situation. The Financial Health Scorecard evaluates size, liquidity, leverage, earnings consistency, dividend record, and valuation ratios.
